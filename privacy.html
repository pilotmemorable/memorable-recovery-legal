Privacy Policy

Memorable Recovery · 2026-11-19

Summary

Memorable Recovery repairs and recovers photos and videos on your own device. Your photos, videos and audio are never uploaded to our servers.

What stays on your device

The files you choose, the repaired and recovered copies, and the app's settings. The original files and cards are never modified.

What we store if you create an account

Your e-mail address, your plan and its expiry, your Apple purchase identifier, and usage records for the free demo (a SHA-256 fingerprint of the file, its type and a device identifier; the fingerprint is not the file). We use them only to run your account, verify your subscription and prevent demo abuse. We do not track you, show ads, sell data or use third-party analytics.

Version 1.0

Version 1.0 works without an account and does not collect any personal data.

Service providers

Supabase (account and database) and Apple (purchases and distribution).

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete the account. In the app choose Account, then Delete account; this removes the account, subscription record and usage records.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, export or deletion (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK and similar laws) by writing to the address below; we answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
