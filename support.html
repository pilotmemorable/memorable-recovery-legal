A video will not play

Choose Repair, pick the folder, then choose the camera that recorded it (for GoPro MAX choose GoPro MAX). Repaired copies are saved next to the originals.

The app says the file holds no camera data

Some files copied from a failing card contain only a controller fill pattern, with no picture data. No tool can repair those. Try recovering from the original card with the Mac card-recovery download.

Deleted files

Recovery of deleted files from a card is in the separate direct download for Mac. Stop using the card first.

Delete my account

In the app: Account, then Delete account.
